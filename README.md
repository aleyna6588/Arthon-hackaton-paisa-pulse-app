# Arthon-hackaton-paisa-pulse-app
PaisaPulse Paste your UPI and bank SMS. Get a budget, catch scams before you tap, and see which tax regime saves you more. 100% in-browser. No backend, no API keys, no data leaves your device.
Features
Budget from SMS: parses UPI/bank debit and credit alerts, auto-categorises merchants (food, groceries, travel, bills and more), shows a spending breakdown, a "money personality", small-payment leak detection and 50/30/20 savings guidance.
Scam Shield: rule-based detector for fake UPI collect requests, OTP/PIN phishing, KYC-block threats, fake jobs, lottery scams, APK/remote-access traps and shortened links. Gives a 0-100 risk score with plain-language explanations.
Tax: old vs new regime: compares both regimes using 80C, 80D, NPS, HRA and home-loan interest, and explains the result in simple words.

Disclaimer
Educational project. Tax figures use FY 2025-26 slabs and ignore surcharge and other income, so verify on incometax.gov.in or with a CA. The scam checker is rule-based and not a guarantee.
